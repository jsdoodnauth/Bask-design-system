Bask is a design system that treats elevation as the primary language. Every interactive surface lives at a known tier — recessed (inputs), flat (rows), raised (cards), floating (overlays) — and the parallax engine threads light through all of them at once.

Motion model

Proximity light. The cursor is a light source with a 380px falloff. Each elevated surface measures its own distance to the cursor and writes --tilt-x --tilt-y on itself, scaled by influence. The card you're looking at gets the full response; cards across the page stay quiet at gravity. The cursor itself is eased frame-to-frame so the shadow never jitters.
